# Grenadier Pool 1998

A retro, top-down pool game inspired by the Grenadier public house, where the creator and his friends played pool in 1998.

## Language

**Persona**:
A friend's representation in the game, using their identity and imagery.

**Computer counterpart**:
A computer-controlled version of a friend's persona with an exaggerated personality, such as angry or cool. This is distinct from the actual friend playing the game.

**Ultimate version**:
A proposed especially skilled computer counterpart of a friend. Its exact difficulty and behavior remain undecided.
