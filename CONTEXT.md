# Grenadier Pool 1998

A retro, top-down pool game inspired by the Grenadier public house, where the creator and his friends played pool in 1998.

## Language

**Persona**:
A friend's representation in the game, using their identity and imagery.

**Computer counterpart**:
A computer-controlled version of a friend's persona with an exaggerated personality, such as angry or cool. This is distinct from the actual friend playing the game.

**Ultimate version**:
A proposed especially skilled computer counterpart of a friend. Its exact difficulty and behavior remain undecided.

**Practice layout**:
A named, repeatable arrangement of the white and three object balls for practising shots without match rules.

**Pot**:
A ball entering a pocket through its mouth and leaving the playing surface.

**Scratch**:
Potting the white. In this practice phase it leads to manual replacement after motion settles, without a foul penalty.

**White placement**:
Choosing a clear playable spot for the white after a scratch. The placement contact finishes before a fresh aiming gesture can begin.

**Table cleared**:
All three object balls have been potted and remaining motion has settled; the player can explicitly re-rack.

**Re-rack**:
Restore the selected practice layout, resetting balls and shot state while retaining the current practice tuning settings within the page.
