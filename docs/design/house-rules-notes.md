## The remembered “two shots” convention

Traditional World Rules award two **visits** after a standard foul. A visit may contain multiple shots while legal pots keep the player at the table. Those rules distinguish potting the black early (loss) from legally potting it after your colours (win), and have break-specific exceptions. These details are not universal rules for every pub. Source: [EPA World 8 Ball Pool Rules, 2019 poster](https://www.epa.org.uk/images/rules/Pool%20Rules%20B3%20poster%20Oct%2019.pdf), sections C, H, K and M; accessed 18 September 2026.

The EPA also documents a different International Eightball ruleset. Choosing “pub rules” does not choose that automatically. Source: [EPA International rules](https://www.epa.org.uk/rules/text_2c.php), section 3 and foul rules; accessed 18 September 2026.

## Proposed casual starting point — not settled

- Clear your group, then legally pot the black to win. Potting it early loses; break/combined-pot/foul-on-black edge cases need explicit rules before implementation.
- Use two visits after the agreed ordinary fouls, with short plain-language feedback. Define the exact foul list and cue-ball placement together; do not silently mix rule families.
- No automatic shot clock by default. If later enabled, warn before expiry and specify whether expiry loses a visit, awards extra visits, or forfeits the frame. A whole-frame forfeit and a two-visit penalty are distinct outcomes, not interchangeable labels.
- Select teammate order for 2 v 2, including how a legal pot and an extra visit affect the next teammate. No default has been approved yet.
- Winner-stays-on needs a challenger queue and clear next-player handover; the earlier 50p detail can be a period table-entry motif. No real payment handling is specified.
- After the final result, knockabout is explicitly unscored and may strike any nominated remaining ball. Re-rack starts a new setup and restores the normal competitive constraints.
