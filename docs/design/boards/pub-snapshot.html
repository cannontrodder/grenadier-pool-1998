Palette + type

Oat paper #EEDFBF, oxblood #7B222A, bottle green #155842, dark timber. Georgia gives an editorial memory voice; Arial carries compact controls at 16px+.

Imagery + texture

Direct-flash crop with imperfect white border; handwriting appears only as brief annotation. Until approved media arrives, every silhouette says “Friend photo placeholder.”

Motion + audio

Persona card settles like a print placed on a table; reactions arrive between shots. Dry cue strike, room hush and one brief cheer, each captioned and independently mutable.

Layout + access

Portrait makes O02 intimate; landscape gives G01 a wider table. Controls stay 44px+, ball marks repeat color, focus is visible, texture never enters the aiming field.

Communicates well

Specific friendship, warmth and a sense of a real evening without inventing biographical details. Best when approved photos and phrases can carry emotional weight.

Fails when

Paper, photos or handwritten jokes cover play; “authentic” grime hurts contrast; people become decorative trophies; nostalgia reads older than 1998.

Recovery states

U01 appears as a slim paper ticket below score, never over balls. X01 uses the same frame with a plain retry; missing portraits collapse to labeled silhouette.

Phone question

Does the warm memory treatment feel personal without making the G01 table harder to read, especially in a dim room and with text enlarged?
