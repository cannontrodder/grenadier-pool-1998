03 / THE DESIGN BRIEF

A specific place, a readable game.

The things we’re keeping

  • Top-down pool, with the table leading the interface.
  • iPhone Safari first; Android remains usable.
  • Friend personas and clearly labeled computer counterparts.
  • A late-1990s character rooted in the pub.
  • Updates that let a player resume the same match.

The things we’re comparing

  • Visual direction and persona treatment.
  • Portrait and landscape table layouts.
  • Aim, power, commit, and cancel controls.
  • How much character belongs between shots.
  • Clear next actions when play is interrupted.
Media and accessibility notes

No real photographs or audio have been supplied. Portraits and reactions are labeled placeholders. The boards use original interface studies, system-font fallbacks, and silent audio references.

Controls should be readable and reachable, feedback should use text as well as color or sound, and the table must remain clear. The preview supports zoom and keyboard navigation; touch feel and physical iPhone behavior still need hands-on review.

04 / WHEN YOU’RE READY TO REVIEW

Look at it on your phone.

  1. Compare A, B, and C. Which feels most like your 1998?
  2. Check the gameplay view. Can you read the table at a glance?
  3. Compare the two layouts. Think about where your thumb rests.
  4. Inspect a persona moment. Does it feel like your friends belong here?
  5. Try the update and restore screens. Is the next action clear?

Any eventual selection will be recorded explicitly. This preview contains no inferred approval and does not settle the pool rules or multiplayer model.
