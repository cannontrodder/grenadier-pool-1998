01 / YOUR SELECTED DIRECTION

Broadcast from the pub.

You chose Sports Broadcast for both the gameplay and the persona moment. The next study keeps that period score-caption and player-profile language, with pixel-art caricatures of your friends, pub light, and brief between-shot scenes giving it a place.

The table takes the screen

No fixed control rail. Compact TV overlays stay away from the finger and the shot, and fade back during interaction.

One finger, one shot

Drag and release, with a way to abort. Rotate the cue around the cue ball; pulling away is a power hypothesis. A separate cue-ball contact selector sets spin/backspin. The exact feel remains for the touch study.

03 / THE DESIGN BRIEF

A specific place, a readable game.

The things we’re keeping

  • Fullscreen top-down pool, with temporary broadcast overlays.
  • iPhone Safari first; Android remains usable.
  • Friend personas and clearly labeled computer counterparts.
  • A late-1990s character rooted in the pub.
  • Updates and reconnects that preserve the current match.

The things still to test

  • Single-finger aim, power, release, and abort feel.
  • Portrait and landscape table layouts.
  • Overlay position, transparency, and finger obstruction.
  • How much character belongs between shots.
  • Clear next actions when play is interrupted.
Media and accessibility notes

The user has supplied a group photo identifying Trod, Craig, Shacka, and Maaaaark. Final voices, individual counterpart traits, sound recordings, and room references remain open. Any schematic people, reactions, or team arrangements are labeled as concepts.

Controls should be readable and reachable, feedback should use text as well as color or sound, and the table must remain clear. The preview supports zoom and keyboard navigation; touch feel and physical iPhone behavior still need hands-on review.
